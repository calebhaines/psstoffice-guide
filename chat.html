Your little signing stamp

An Ed25519 key gives your notes a recognizable signing identity. A valid signature proves control of that key, not a real-world identity. Your private key stays in page memory and is cleared when you leave or close the page.

To reuse your identity, deliberately download the private key and keep that file secret. It is unencrypted; anyone with the file can sign as this key. Nothing is saved automatically.
